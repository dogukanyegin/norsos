Norsos Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026
Summary: Norsos is designed as a local-first emergency/SOS application. It does not require an account and does not contain advertising or advertising trackers. Emergency contacts and app settings are stored on the user's device.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Norsos handles information when you use the Android application. Norsos provides SOS, emergency-contact, location, siren, flashlight, vibration, calling and messaging-assistance features.

2. Information handled by the app

Norsos may handle the following information on your device:

  • Emergency contact information: names, phone numbers, relationship labels and primary-contact status entered by you.
  • Location data: approximate or precise device coordinates when location permission is granted.
  • App settings: language, SOS message template, countdown settings, siren, flashlight, vibration, test-mode and related preferences.

3. How information is used

Emergency contact information is used to prepare calls or messages to the contacts you select. Location data is used to create location text or a map link that you can include in an emergency message. App settings are used to provide your selected behavior and language preferences.

4. Storage

Norsos stores emergency contacts and application settings locally on the device. Norsos does not require you to create a Norsos account.

5. Sharing and external apps

Norsos uses Android system intents to open compatible phone, messaging or sharing applications. When you choose to send an emergency message, place a call or share information, the external application or service you select may process that information under its own privacy policy.

A generated map link may open an external mapping service. Use of that service is governed by the privacy terms of the selected mapping provider.

6. Permissions

  • Location: used to obtain coordinates for emergency-location features.
  • Camera: used only to control the device flashlight/torch where supported. Norsos does not use this permission to take photos or record video.
  • Vibration: used for SOS haptic feedback.
  • Internet and network state: may be available to the application for network-dependent components or platform functionality.

Calling and SMS actions are initiated through standard Android system intents such as ACTION_DIAL and ACTION_SENDTO. Norsos does not require the restricted CALL_PHONE or SEND_SMS permissions for these actions.

7. Advertising and tracking

Norsos does not display advertising and is not designed to use advertising identifiers for behavioral advertising.

8. Data deletion

You can delete locally stored Norsos data using the application's reset/delete-data controls, where available. Uninstalling the application removes its local application data from the device, subject to Android and device backup/restore behavior.

9. Children

Norsos is a general-purpose safety utility. It is not designed to collect personal information from children through a Norsos account or server-based profile. Parents or guardians should supervise use where appropriate.

10. Security

Norsos is designed to minimize data handling by keeping core user-entered information on the device. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Third-party services

Norsos may rely on Android operating-system components and external applications chosen by the user, including phone, SMS, sharing and mapping applications. Those third parties have their own privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised version is published.

13. Contact

For privacy questions relating to Norsos, contact the developer through the support contact published on the Norsos Google Play listing or the project's official GitHub repository.
